01 About

About.

A knitwear designer with a sportswear-for-training specialisation from NIFT Delhi. The practice balances aesthetics with functionality across performance wear, athleisure & lifestyle apparel — bringing athletic-grade fabric thinking to a contemporary brief.

Strong end-to-end ownership: concept ideation, design detailing, tech pack development, sampling coordination — through to a finished prototype that holds up against fabric weight, print fastness, and production scalability.
